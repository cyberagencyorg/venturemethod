# Project positioning and public copy

## Working identity

**Name:** Venture Method

**Creator:** Brad Khan

**Descriptor:** An agent-agnostic framework for research, strategy, product, ventures and evidence-linked decisions.

**Tagline:** Investigate deeply. Choose your methods. Make decisions you can revisit.

**GitHub About field (suggested):** A portable framework for applying rigorous methods, tracing decisions to evidence, and maintaining living knowledge across ventures, products, and organizations.

**Audience:** Founders and solo venture architects first. Product leaders, executives, advisors, family offices, and venture studios as the work and benchmarks mature.

**Key distinction:** Not another universal management methodology and not simply a large list of AI skills. A method-aware, evidence-linked framework whose purpose is to improve cross-disciplinary judgment and keep its reasoning usable over time.

## Short description

A useful venture or product decision often draws on market research, customer discovery, strategy, operating models, finance, and execution. Those activities rarely belong to one discipline, and their outputs become stale quickly. Venture Method connects the source material, methods, decisions and follow-on work so people and agents can work rigorously without rebuilding the entire process each time.

## Shareable introduction

Most AI workflows are good at making a document. The harder question is whether the document is based on the right research, the right method, and assumptions that still hold six weeks later.

Venture Method is a portable framework being developed to address that problem. It connects research, strategic methods, specialist agent capabilities, decisions, and ongoing revision. Bring a company, market, venture, product problem or client mandate. The system helps structure the work, preserve the reasoning, and revisit what changes.

The current release is a local prototype, with a working decision record, method catalog, Opportunity Solution Tree and fictional end-to-end demonstration. It is designed for ambitious future uses but does not yet autonomously research companies or replace professional diligence.

## Presentation guidance

- Demonstrate a live revision and its affected decision references before showing the framework catalog. That is the concrete differentiator.
- Explain a method by showing why it is appropriate for a particular question, not by celebrating the number of methods.
- Compare a brief with an OST or financial hypothesis as different views of the same underlying engagement, while being explicit about what is and is not connected in the current code.
- Avoid claims about outperforming venture funds, replacing executive teams, or achieving professional quality before independent evaluations exist.
- Do not claim the repository is open-source until an approved public license is included. A public project still needs an owner-approved redistribution license.
- Avoid screenshots of synthetic research without clear synthetic labels.

## Future-proofing

Brand, domain and portal decisions are downstream. The framework must remain useful locally and under different organizations' brands and tools. The repository should not depend on a particular account, personal domain, agent runtime, or dashboard.
