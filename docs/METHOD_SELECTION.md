# Method selection: protocol, not a shopping list

The purpose of the method catalog is to help decide **which intellectual work is warranted**, not to maximize the number of frameworks applied to an engagement.

## The practical sequence

1. **Name the decision.** What will change as a result of this work, who owns that choice, and when is a decision needed?
2. **Classify the uncertainty.** Is the central question about customer needs, market structure, economic viability, feasibility, organizational capability, capital allocation, or future conditions? It may span several.
3. **Establish evidence readiness.** Distinguish unresearched assumptions, provisional reports, observations and independently corroborated material. Evidence quality is multidimensional; the current four-label selector is a convenience, not an epistemic truth score.
4. **Identify candidate methods.** For each, record which question it answers, required inputs, known limitations, output, effort, and what would invalidate its use.
5. **Prefer a minimum sufficient combination.** Use complementary methods where each contributes distinct decision value. Avoid applying overlapping frameworks solely because they are available.
6. **Execute and challenge.** Apply the method with competent sources and domain expertise. Have someone challenge selection, interpretation, rival explanations and omitted alternatives.
7. **Keep the result revisable.** Attach the source snapshot, method version and decision context. Define triggers for revisiting the work.

## Example: an unfamiliar B2B service company

**Mandate:** Should an owner invest in software to improve customer retention?

A poor workflow begins with a software PRD. A better workflow begins by finding out whether retention is truly a problem, how it is measured, and what alternatives exist.

Potential method sequence:

- **Company and operating-system diagnosis:** Inspect actual retention, workflows and constraints. Do not assume a product intervention.
- **Customer research / JTBD:** Understand why accounts reduce or discontinue usage. Distinguish switching, budget pressure, customer service, price and product utility.
- **Opportunity Solution Tree, only if warranted:** When there is a focused product outcome and real customer evidence, map opportunities, alternatives and assumption tests.
- **Unit economics / financial scenarios:** Compare the cost and expected benefits of staffing, process, partnership and software options.
- **Decision analysis:** Record reversible experiments, downside, alternatives and evidence that would cause reconsideration.

The output may be a simple process change, a pilot, a recommendation to do nothing, or a product initiative. The method is not chosen merely because the user mentioned software.

## Meaningful distinctions

- **Torres / continuous discovery:** Repeated product learning anchored to customer evidence and a defined outcome. Not a one-time company assessment.
- **Opportunity Solution Tree:** Outcome, customer opportunities, solutions, assumption tests. A focused product discovery representation, not a universal portfolio pipeline.
- **Marty Cagan / product operating model:** Organization design, competencies, coaching, decision rights and empowered teams. Not a PRD template.
- **Strategyzer / canvases:** Connected business-model or value-proposition hypotheses. Not proof of product-market fit.
- **Wardley Mapping:** User needs, value chain, evolution and strategic movement. Not automatically a competitor feature grid.
- **Qualitative JTBD vs Outcome-Driven Innovation:** Related approaches with different questions, data collection methods and analysis requirements. Do not collapse them into one generic instruction.
- **Scenario planning vs forecasts:** Plausible alternative futures are not point predictions.
- **Real options vs simple ROI:** Staged and reversible investments can be valuable under uncertainty, but valuation demands economic assumptions and suitable analytical competence.

## Present capability

`python vm.py frameworks --goal GOAL --evidence EVIDENCE` compares records in `config/frameworks-v3.json` and exposes fit, caution and input readiness. It **does not** evaluate the decision economics, run interviews, select automatically, or learn routing quality from outcomes. Those are development goals with explicit evaluation needs.

New method candidates must carry a credible source, prerequisites, outputs, failure conditions, adaptation rights, and a test case that demonstrates value beyond existing methods. No framework receives authority from popularity alone.
