# Marketing, brand and go-to-market specialist adoption

**Added:** 28 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Curated external methods for projects such as Cyber Agency, not a proposal to remake Venture Method as a full marketing automation suite.  
**Status:** Candidate integrations, not installed, pinned, executed, evaluated or endorsed as producing superior results.

## Reuse the skills already in Venture Method

Read and apply `marketing-context`, `competitive-positioning`, `brand-architecture`, `marketing-copywriting`, `brand-voice-preservation`, `human-writing-audit` and `market-landscape` (where relevant). These cover the company-level decisions and substantive writing reviews that are especially easy to lose when switching agents. The host project retains final authority over decisions, copy, customer evidence and approvals.

**Critical brand identity exception:** the host project's latest explicit owner rejection/supersession can override an older supposedly immutable kit. Reuse approved original artwork only when *currently* approved; do not treat old assets as permanent authority. Cyber Agency's October 2026 rebrand demonstrates why this is necessary.

## Best-fit external specialist libraries to consult

**Primary:** Corey Haines' Marketing Skills, https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills . As of 28 October 2026 its documentation reports an MIT license and skills including `customer-research`, `offers`, `pricing`, `site-architecture`, `content-strategy`, `cro`, `prospecting`, `cold-email`, `sales-enablement`, `revops`, `seo-audit`, `analytics`, `conversion-tracking`, `schema`, `ai-seo`, `ab-testing` and `product-marketing`. Skill folder names have changed in v2; verify the exact revision rather than assuming cached names. These are user-invoked specialist procedures, not validated agents that may send email, pay for software, modify sites or access accounts autonomously.

**Human-language QA:** https://github.com/blader/humanizer , source inspiration for the existing `human-writing-audit` skill. Do not create a second generic 'humanizer' without a measured benefit.

**Positioning:** April Dunford's `Obviously Awesome` alternative-first category/value method. VM's `competitive-positioning` already contains a bounded interpretation. New value comes from actual lost-deal/customer evidence, not another template.

**Demand and editorial:** B2B buying-committee content design as articulated by Ahrefs (https://ahrefs.com/blog/b2b-content-marketing/) and Google's helpful, original, people-first content guidance (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content). The user-facing artifact is a rigorous editorial plan tied to real customer questions, not a manufactured funnel diagram.

**Premium capability presentation examples:** Reaktor (https://www.reaktor.com/services), Trail of Bits (https://trailofbits.com/services), IDEO (https://www.ideo.com/practices), Palantir's AIP Bootcamp (https://www.palantir.com/platforms/aip/bootcamp/), Work & Co (https://www.work.co/). Study information architecture, content-proving patterns, buying journeys and original research; do not reproduce distinctive copy, protected images, customer evidence or layouts.

## Integration protocol

1. Host supplies scope, authority, previously approved brand, negative decisions and actual buyer evidence.
2. Venture Method selects one method or named specialist skill; don't load 20 generic marketing skills into every engagement.
3. Review source commit/version, license, references and *instructions that could cause external side effects*, particularly scraping, bulk email or account access.
4. Consult or install only after user authorization; do not copy dependencies or templates without rights. Prefer read-only consulting of external skill when that suffices.
5. Record the exact output contract, claim boundaries, required human approvals and skill version.
6. Compare representative and adversarial outputs against no-skill baseline: relevance, factuality, buyer specificity, language originality, risk and time saved.
7. Preserve successful resulting work in the host project and record any useful VM adaptation separately. An increased skill count or a green structural test is not proof of expert marketing competence.
