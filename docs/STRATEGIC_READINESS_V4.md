# Venture Architecture v0.4: adversarial readiness and strategic benchmark

## Explicit assessment

**Not yet world-class.** v0.4 is a tested offline scaffolding and a limited discovery subdomain, not a venture studio network platform and not an autonomous fractional CPO. The original goal remains sound but proof requires real deployments, quality comparisons, domain-specific analyses and professional review. This scorecard is a qualitative risk assessment, not a benchmark result.

| Dimension | Current state | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market, customer and product analysis | Sparse SKILL.md guidance; no automated research ingestion | Real sourced competitive dossiers, segmented TAM/SAM/SOM, customer evidence, falsification exercises, maintained OSTs |
| Business/venture architecture | Broad framework catalog and isolated engagement records | Repeatable venture thesis, business model, GTM, finance, risk, capital pathways across 3 countries |
| Multi-country origination | Planned only | Lead sourcing, jurisdiction/actor resolution, screening, partner diligence and relevant financing by market |
| Investor network advantage | Not implemented | Accessible operator/founder/investor graph, warm-introduction consent, unique opportunity sources, measurable conversion and outcomes |
| Fractional executive work | Method metadata and basic artifacts | First-30-days assessment, roadmap, real decision-rights map, cross-team operations, product metrics and board updates |
| Knowledge continuity | Hash-chain record journals, claim revision proposals, preliminary OST changes | Database transactions, stable IDs, semantic diffs, review gates, dependency impact, re-evaluation and artifact lineage |
| Agent interoperability | Portable skill formats, local CLI | Hermes + at least 2 other adapters pass same task suite; authenticated API and tool-level permissions |
| Artifact excellence | Local Markdown drafts only | Professionally assessed docs, models, charts, decks and client communications with factual and design QA |
| Client/partner security | Gitignored local data, no auth or multi-user | Enforced tenants, RBAC/ABAC, encrypted data, audit, backup/restore, penetration review |
| Evaluation | Unit/structural tests; skill eval fixtures | Blinded expert review, live multiple-model runs, no-skill comparisons, costs, adverse cases and measured outcomes |

## Five practical benchmark engagements

1. **Solo venture architect:** Identify an underserved B2B logistics problem in a less-digitized regional market. Produce a credible, cited market map, customer discovery plan, enterprise service/software alternatives, unit economics and go/no-go criteria. Measure hours saved and accuracy versus competent analyst baseline.
2. **Fractional CPO:** Enter a mid-size SaaS company with low retention and unclear product ownership. Deliver a 30-day diagnosis, product outcome framing, interview program, opportunity tree, capacity-conscious roadmap, measurable product operating cadence, executive discussion plan and decision records. Compare to professional CPO assessment.
3. **Studio portfolio:** Operate a network of 10 fictional ventures in 5 jurisdictions, with shared vendors/investors but isolated confidential files, overlapping partner contacts, contradictory data and funding changes. No client-to-client leakage. Review portfolio concentration, resource allocation and decision histories.
4. **Diligence:** Evaluate a cross-border partnership and proposed investment with sparse information and regulatory uncertainty. Distinguish knowns, unverifiable claims, legal questions, downside and staged capital commitments. An expert reviewer must be able to reproduce reasoning.
5. **Bounded client delivery:** Produce one exceptional marketing or service strategy artifact, choosing a short process and declining irrelevant methods. The system should not force a venture thesis or discovery tree onto every engagement.

Benchmark dimensions: factual precision, evidence coverage and independence, reasoning quality, alternative exploration, numerical validity, commercial viability, operational usefulness, client empathy, privacy/security, iteration behavior, time, cost and unnecessary process. Expert graders should be blinded to treatment. A strong outcome is not simply more artifacts.

## Missing network capabilities

- **Origination:** company/founder/project discovery, local institutions, partner channels, procurement, grants, concessional finance, customers, investors and acquirers.
- **Relationship and entity intelligence:** multilingual aliases, beneficial ownership, legal entities versus brands, confidence, provenance, explicit permission before outreach.
- **Venture manufacturing:** repeated validation, operator matching, venture design, distribution, R&D, capital design, incubation and growth interventions.
- **Capital intelligence:** instrument type and terms, eligibility, geography, deadlines, restrictions, equity dilution, FX exposure, grant status, milestones and compliance; sources must be current and independently checked.
- **Portfolio governance:** stage gates, experiments, outcome and risk metrics, resource allocation, conflict handling, lessons and capital return; a general PM dashboard cannot substitute.
- **Global South competitiveness:** context-specific research in relevant languages, institutional realities, connectivity, purchasing power, payment rails, distribution, regulation, local ownership and practical partnership structures. Do not treat regions as uniform markets.

Competing with established VC is not achieved by a superior prompt library. It depends on proprietary access to worthwhile opportunities, local trust, distribution, capital relationships, decision quality, execution outcomes and reputation. The system can multiply these assets but cannot conjure them.

## Architecture review rules

- Never confuse a cited method with implemented ability.
- Never claim a framework works in every jurisdiction, industry or team shape.
- Do not auto-select famous frameworks merely because they are popular.
- Treat objections as falsifiable checks against actual implementation, not an invitation to abandon sound architectural choices.
- Require at least two counterfactual alternatives, including defer/do nothing where relevant.
- Capture cost of method application, not just theoretical benefits.
- Test recursive improvement against independent cases; don't let agents grade only their own work.
- Skill packages, theories, method selection metadata, domain entities and rendered deliverables must remain separately versioned objects.
- Prefer one rich, coherent knowledge system that can produce many views, while preserving scope, permissions and distinct organizational contexts.

## Explicit v0.4 limitations

The OST CLI uses self-asserted reviewer names, not authentication. It checks source *type* and claim *existence*, not interview truth or quality. It supports local single-writer change proposals, not collaboration or automatically propagated artifact diffs. The common cross-client/portfolio graph, API, portal, financial modelling and research ingestion do not exist yet.
