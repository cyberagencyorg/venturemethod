# Questions for Tahir

These are designed to distinguish strategy, current fact and testable hypothesis without assuming that absence from one meeting means absence company-wide.

## Product and customer

1. What is the single narrow job Gate must complete at launch, and what observable user outcome defines success?
2. Which risky coding-agent actions occur frequently enough that developers actively want a gate, rather than accepting native confirmations or sandboxing?
3. Who is the first specific target segment: internal product teams, outsourced development firms, regulated enterprises, AI-native startups, or another group?
4. For the Vietnamese dev-house problem (00:37:04–00:38:45), who owns the pain, how urgent is it, what is the current workaround, and has anyone agreed to a pilot or price?
5. What did the two external setup attempts actually achieve? For each, is status POC, pilot, production, paying, retained and referenceable?
6. What customer evidence supports the assumed path from an individual install to a Team or Enterprise purchase?
7. What would falsify the Gate thesis within four to six weeks?

## Product experience and measurement

8. What are the launch targets for install success, time to first governed action, time to first proof, weekly retention, false blocks, approval completion and teammate invitation?
9. What exact “aha” use case will the first cohort run, and can it be completed without OpenBox staff?
10. How will you distinguish download, install, login, successful governed session, repeat use, active team and paying account?
11. Who owns instrumentation and a weekly learning review, and what decisions will each metric trigger?
12. What evidence would justify charging after 30 days, and what value disappears or remains in Free?

## Architecture and security

13. Which coding agents and tool calls are supported at launch, and what happens for an unsupported action?
14. What are the measured decision latency, availability and offline behavior of fail-closed enforcement?
15. How are PEP bypass, policy tampering, local cache freshness, key management and proof replay handled?
16. Who can independently verify a Proof Certificate, and what trust assumption remains in OpenBox's proprietary service?
17. Which PEP/verdict/certificate interfaces are still unstable, and what conformance tests must pass before open source?
18. What security review, penetration test, disclosure process and incident-response capability exist before marketing Gate as security?
19. What was required to build the custom customer SDK in one week, and how much of that effort repeats per framework/customer?
