## Proposed backlog/release work

- Billing, usage metering and entitlements sufficient for Free/Team/Enterprise conversion (00:24:46–00:25:21; 00:29:45–00:30:15).
- Gate product page, documentation, explainer/video assets, and progressively deeper technical tutorials (00:25:58–00:26:35; 01:01:48–01:03:09).
- One-line or near-one-line coding-agent plugin install, a narrow first task, and visible audit/playback/proof (01:24:32–01:27:16).
- Framework/product-specific pages rather than a generic integrations page (00:23:08–00:24:25).
- Complete the two active external integrations and turn them into credible use cases (00:42:39–00:47:47).
- Stabilize and decouple the PEP before an open-source release; scrub history/secrets; add security and conformance assets ([00:06:30](../media/frames/frame-00-06-30.jpg)).
- Productized natural-language policy authoring and post-session security review were discussed as differentiators, but no acceptance tests or owner were recorded (00:14:14–00:15:30).

## Explicit or implicit “not now” choices

- Do not prioritize the undifferentiated full enterprise platform as the near-term November revenue path (00:29:45–00:30:36).
- Do not target solo developers as the core economic buyer; target developers inside credible organizations who can expand to teams (00:51:38–00:52:58; 01:19:01–01:20:49).
- Do not open-source proprietary decision/control/verification layers, and do not publish an unstable PEP API ([00:05:30](../media/frames/frame-00-05-30.jpg); [00:06:30](../media/frames/frame-00-06-30.jpg)).
- Do not attempt to redesign and launch the entire website in one overnight change; add product pages incrementally (00:02:39–00:03:15).
