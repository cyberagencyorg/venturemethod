## Visual coverage limitations

- Screenshares are concentrated in approximately 00:01:25–00:43:00. From about 00:44:00 onward, the recording is predominantly participant tiles, so later evidence is chiefly audio/transcript based.
- The feature inventory, landing pages, tables, and strategy documents are assertions/mockups shown by the team. No source code, Jira backlog, test run, production dashboard, billing flow, live AWS transaction, or end-to-end customer deployment was demonstrated.
- Participant names were visible, but active-speaker sampling every 30 seconds is insufficient for reliable utterance-by-utterance diarization. The transcript therefore avoids broad name attribution.
