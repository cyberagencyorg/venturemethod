## Target users and buyers discussed

- **Initial user:** developer using Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or another coding agent, especially inside a larger company (00:51:38–00:52:58; 01:25:27–01:25:58).
- **Team buyer/champion:** engineering manager/director, VP Engineering, AI leader or a developer who expands adoption; expected to value productivity plus secure standards (00:55:13–00:58:37).
- **Control stakeholders:** security, governance, compliance and sometimes legal; may influence or own budget depending on organization (00:53:19–00:58:37).
- **Enterprise procurement path:** direct/custom or AWS Marketplace, with enterprise controls and on-prem options (00:00:38–00:03:14; 00:40:24–00:42:32).

## Evidence-integrity cautions

- The meeting moves at times from “attempted setup” to “live use case” language without resolving production status (00:42:39–00:47:47).
- Proposed testimonials may be paid, employee-recruited, or portfolio-sourced. Those facts do not invalidate feedback, but external representations should disclose the relationship and distinguish trial, active use, production use and paying customer (00:58:41–01:04:04; 01:19:01–01:24:31).
- The AWS page and Gate page are mockups. They demonstrate positioning, not marketplace availability or working product behavior ([AWS](../media/frames/frame-00-02-00.jpg); [Gate](../media/frames/frame-00-10-45.jpg)).
