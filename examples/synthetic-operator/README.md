# Fictional service-operations engagement

This is an intentionally synthetic, non-client example. **None of its market, operational, interview or company claims refer to a real business.**

**Input:** Evaluate a manual quoting workflow without assuming the answer is software.

**Artifact:** An evidence-linked, unpublished draft showing one claim, one hypothesis, a low-cost option, a draft decision, a reviewer limitation, and an explicit do-nothing baseline. The reference trace links the decision back to its claim and a hypothetical source. The record-check artifact is a structural review only.

Files:

- `route-initial.json`: first routing attempt, retained as a regression example; it over-selected domains due to simplistic keyword matching.
- `route-refined.json`: improved route prioritizing actual operational diagnosis over mere file-format words and incidental references to software.
- `provenance-trace.json`: source/claim/decision/review graph traversal as JSON.
- `decision-brief.md`: private local draft example, source verification not attempted.
- `structural-check.json`: successful referential and journal checks, **not** factual or business-quality certification.

## Important limitations

The fictional reviewer typed into the CLI is not cryptographically authenticated. The asserted staff account is not a real interview. This exercise proves reproducible record linking and offline artifact assembly, not the correctness of venture strategy, market forecasts, customer research, or AI skill effectiveness. Real research collection, independent review, permissions, document publishing and agent execution are future adapters.
