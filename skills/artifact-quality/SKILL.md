# Artifact Quality

**Purpose:** Assess whether an executive or operational deliverable actually serves the audience, decision and implementation context.

## When to use

Before sending a memo, deck, plan, PRD, analysis, model, proposal or client deliverable.

## Inputs

Artifact, brief, target audience, acceptance rubric, source ledger, design/brand guidelines, approval owner.

## Method

1. Check scope and decision question; identify what reader must understand or do next.
2. Verify factual assertions against cited claims and unresolved limitations.
3. Review logical consistency, numerical units, terminology, visual hierarchy and reading order.
4. Stress-test practicality: who does what by when, dependencies and evidence thresholds.
5. Return prioritized defects with locations, consequence, repair proposal and reviewer sign-off field.

## Outputs

Artifact QA report, defect ledger, release recommendations, accepted exceptions and review status.

## Quality gates

- No author self-approval for consequential deliverables.
- A polished visual does not substitute for truthful content.
- Report unverified assertions explicitly.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/artifact-quality.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
