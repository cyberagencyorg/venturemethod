# Capital Landscape

**Purpose:** Identify and compare capital sources and financing instruments for a venture or operating company.

## When to use

A project needs grants, public programs, non-dilutive support, debt, strategic investment, equity, incubators, credits, or local incentives.

## Inputs

Entity jurisdiction, incorporation, founders/ownership, sector, project phase, investment use, eligible geography, timing, prohibited terms, target financing.

## Method

1. Generate a global discovery taxonomy: public programs, multilateral agencies, foundations, academic, municipal/regional, corporate, venture, growth, impact and private financing.
2. Classify every opportunity by capital instrument, cash/noncash benefit, dilution, repayment, matching, IP obligations, reporting and geographic restrictions.
3. For each program retrieve primary administrator documents, deadlines and amendments; record accessed and effective dates separately.
4. Check legal entity and team eligibility, physical presence, sector fit, founders, minimum funding, stage and ownership exclusions.
5. Distinguish currently open calls from evergreen programs, rumors and historical cohorts; flag uncertain terms.
6. Rank expected net capital value after compliance costs, opportunity costs, dilution, timing and probability rather than headline awards.
7. Propose monitored refresh queues; no automatic submission, certification or legal assertion.

## Outputs

Funding opportunity register; eligibility matrix; instrument economics; watchlist query plan; application calendar; diligence uncertainties.

## Quality gates

- No program is labeled open or eligible without current primary-source confirmation.
- Do not mistake credits/services for cash or loans for grants.
- Include jurisdiction and residency restrictions.
- No external applications or disclosures without explicit permission.
- Prefer evidence-linked, economical work proportionate to actual decision risk.

## Handoff contract

State engagement ID, bounded objective, source and claim IDs, open assumptions, options, reviewer, approval owner, and next test.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/capital-landscape.json` with representative, ambiguous, and adversarial cases against a no-skill baseline. Structural checks alone are not effectiveness proof.
