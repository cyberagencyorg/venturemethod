# Institutional Landscape

**Purpose:** Understand regulators, counterparties, policy incentives, authorities, stakeholders, and institutional dependencies.

## When to use

A venture, client, industry or public-interest project depends on government, regulatory approvals, procurement, grants, networks or ecosystem formation.

## Inputs

Jurisdiction and date, entity types, institutions and mandates, enforcement data, stakeholder interests, legal advice access, public documents.

## Method

1. Resolve jurisdictions, authorities, statutory and non-statutory roles, legal bodies and date of effect.
2. Map stakeholders, legitimate interests, incentives, opposition, influence and accountability with sourced supporting observations.
3. Identify licenses, approvals, public procurement constraints, local incentives, residency and sector-specific qualifications.
4. Compare documented government policy to operating reality without assuming published intent equals practical availability.
5. Assess routes for compliant partnership, public benefit, procurement, advocacy, risk mitigation and institutional redesign.
6. Document legal questions requiring qualified counsel and update cadence for changing rules.

## Outputs

Institutional actor map; regulatory and policy question register; partnership options; risk matrix; official-source refresh plan.

## Quality gates

- Distinguish current binding requirements from guidance, proposals and old rules.
- No unsourced motive attribution, improper pressure or evasion of official procedures.
- Separate association from control or influence.
- Legal/compliance interpretations require professional review.
- Prefer evidence-linked, economical work proportionate to actual decision risk.

## Handoff contract

State engagement ID, bounded objective, source and claim IDs, open assumptions, options, reviewer, approval owner, and next test.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/institutional-landscape.json` with representative, ambiguous, and adversarial cases against a no-skill baseline. Structural checks alone are not effectiveness proof.
