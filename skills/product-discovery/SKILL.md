# Product Discovery

**Purpose:** Guide ongoing research to identify customer needs and de-risk value, usability, feasibility and business viability.

## When to use

A product or service is being conceived, improved, repositioned or assessed for investment.

## Inputs

Research notes, target segment, target outcome, usage patterns, interview access, analytics, constraints.

## Method

1. Define a measurable customer/business outcome and target customer segment.
2. Prioritize story-based interviews about past behavior and existing workarounds, not leading purchase-intent questions.
3. Synthesize opportunity groups and specify evidence counts and counterexamples.
4. Generate multiple solutions and test their riskiest assumptions separately.
5. Recommend a continuous discovery cadence with owners, capture, changes and follow-up.

## Outputs

Discovery plan, interview guide, opportunity hierarchy, assumptions map and learning log.

## Quality gates

- Do not invent interviews, quotations or metrics.
- Declare selection and confirmation bias.
- Avoid universal claims from a few interviews.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/product-discovery.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
