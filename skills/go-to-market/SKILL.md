# Go To Market

**Purpose:** Design an evidence-based route from a differentiated offer to reachable buyers and repeatable revenue.

## When to use

A venture or client needs commercial entry, acquisition, distribution, pricing tests or launch sequencing.

## Inputs

Defined buyer segments, verified/claimed pain, value proposition, purchase process, geography, channel constraints, competitor substitutes, budget.

## Method

1. Separate total category demand, serviceable buyers, reachable buyers and near-term buying intent. Do not infer market size from search traffic.
2. Map buying committee, triggers, procurement friction, legal/geographic constraints, existing behaviors and viable substitutes.
3. Generate channel hypotheses including founder-led sales, partners, inbound, outbound, marketplace, community and distribution via incumbents; exclude infeasible channels.
4. Estimate acquisition economics with assumption ranges and cost drivers; keep observed conversion separate from hypothetical targets.
5. Choose the cheapest credible experiments to test message-market resonance, reachability and willingness to pay before scaling spend.
6. Define sequencing, owners, budgets, thresholds and pivot/stop conditions.

## Outputs

Segment and ICP table; positioning hypotheses; competitive alternatives; channel experiment plan; GTM financial assumptions; launch readiness memo.

## Quality gates

- No unsupported TAM, CAC, conversion, retention or pricing numbers.
- Differentiate awareness from purchase intent and experiments from observed results.
- Show realistic acquisition and fulfillment constraints.
- Label what requires customer, channel and sales-system verification.
- Prefer evidence-linked, economical work proportionate to actual decision risk.

## Handoff contract

State engagement ID, bounded objective, source and claim IDs, open assumptions, options, reviewer, approval owner, and next test.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/go-to-market.json` with representative, ambiguous, and adversarial cases against a no-skill baseline. Structural checks alone are not effectiveness proof.
