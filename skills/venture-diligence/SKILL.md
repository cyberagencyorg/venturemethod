# Venture Diligence

**Purpose:** Run disciplined opportunity, company and transaction diligence with independent evidence, downside scenarios and decision criteria.

## When to use

Investment consideration, M&A, JV, fundraising, venture partnership or high-cost commitment.

## Inputs

Transaction terms, entity identity, financials, operating data, market facts, legal constraints, access/permission boundaries.

## Method

1. Confirm deal structure, instrument, ownership, rights, dilution, economics and decision authority.
2. Maintain separate workstreams: commercial, technical, financial, operational, people, legal and regulatory.
3. Test key founder/management assertions against primary evidence and independent alternatives.
4. Build red-flag register, downside scenario, concentration risks and validation requests.
5. Produce explicit proceed/renegotiate/defer/stop recommendation with missing evidence and owner.

## Outputs

Diligence memo, source register, request list, transaction issues table, thesis risks, decision recommendation.

## Quality gates

- No claim of legal or investment approval.
- Never invent cap table or financial statements.
- High-stakes decisions require qualified review.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/venture-diligence.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
