# Operating Model

**Purpose:** Translate strategy into capabilities, roles, governance, processes, measurement and accountable delivery mechanisms.

## When to use

Designing or transforming a company, business unit, consultancy, portfolio company or product organization.

## Inputs

Strategy, value chains, operating constraints, org structure, budgets, processes, role owners and technology landscape.

## Method

1. Map value delivery from demand through execution to outcomes; document current versus desired capabilities.
2. Identify decision rights, process ownership, collaboration and escalation rules separately from job titles.
3. Map systems, data dependencies, capacity constraints and policy requirements.
4. Design governance at the lowest complexity sufficient for risk; choose meaningful metrics and review cadence.
5. Define migration sequencing, adoption, training, operational exceptions and feedback collection.

## Outputs

Capability map, RACI or decision-rights model, target operating model, transformation increments and KPI definitions.

## Quality gates

- Avoid assigning responsibility to nonexistent roles.
- Do not prescribe organizational restructuring without evidence.
- Distinguish ideal-state design from approved organizational change.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.
