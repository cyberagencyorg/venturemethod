# Opportunity Discovery

**Purpose:** Discover unmet needs and systemic opportunities before overcommitting to a solution or venture thesis.

## When to use

Exploring new ventures, products, services, organizational improvements or market whitespace.

## Inputs

Target actors, customer stories or interview evidence, outcomes, incumbent alternatives, observed behaviors.

## Method

1. State beneficiary, desired progress and measurable outcome.
2. Distinguish observed unmet needs from founder ideas and untested assumptions.
3. Group needs into an opportunity hierarchy without forcing every claim into a preconceived feature.
4. Generate a diverse solution set, including services, process redesign, partnership, acquisition and software.
5. Rank tests by expected information gain, cost, speed and ethical constraints.

## Outputs

Opportunity hierarchy, customer-job map, testable opportunity statements and next experiments.

## Quality gates

- Do not fabricate customer interview findings.
- A diagram assembled from assumptions must be labeled provisional.
- Do not prematurely fix the intervention type.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/opportunity-discovery.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
