# Commercial Offer

**Purpose:** Turn strategic capacity into a buyer-ready commercial offer with bounded scope, pricing, success and delivery economics.

## When to use

Preparing advisory services, retainers, productized services, proposals, partnerships, agency deliverables or recurring revenue models.

## Inputs

Buyer, measurable problem, team capacity, exclusions, rate/cost assumptions, implementation risks, terms, evidence and desired economics.

## Method

1. Identify the buying occasion and economically meaningful buyer outcome.
2. Translate capabilities into verifiable deliverables and mutually understood acceptance criteria.
3. Choose appropriate structure among assessment, sprint, implementation, retainer, subscription, performance-linked or hybrid.
4. Calculate delivery effort, gross margin, scope risk, renewal/expansion options, dependency and liability.
5. Develop at least two price/packaging alternatives and document what each requires from the client.
6. Produce a proposal with clear change control, risks, approval path and measurable checkpoints.

## Outputs

Offer brief; scope of work; package and price options; effort/margin assumptions; proposal skeleton; acceptance criteria.

## Quality gates

- No guarantee of business results without credible evidence.
- Price/margin calculations disclose assumptions.
- Do not promise capabilities that do not exist.
- Commercial terms and approvals are human-owned.
- Prefer evidence-linked, economical work proportionate to actual decision risk.

## Handoff contract

State engagement ID, bounded objective, source and claim IDs, open assumptions, options, reviewer, approval owner, and next test.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/commercial-offer.json` with representative, ambiguous, and adversarial cases against a no-skill baseline. Structural checks alone are not effectiveness proof.
