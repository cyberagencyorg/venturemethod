# Company Investigation

**Purpose:** Develop a grounded company dossier with business mechanics, people, customers, competition, finances, and unresolved questions.

## When to use

Investigating a prospective client, partner, employer, acquisition target, investment, competitor or operating company.

## Inputs

Legal and trade names, geographies, sites, filings, public/private status, industry, investor context, reference date, source access.

## Method

1. Resolve entity identity, subsidiaries, brand aliases, ownership and jurisdictions before combining records.
2. Map products, ICPs, customer jobs, channels, partnerships, revenue mechanisms and cost drivers.
3. Trace funding, financial claims, leadership and timelines with original sources and dated access records.
4. Use competitors and substitutes to test positioning, not only management marketing copy.
5. Surface contradictions, missing evidence, incentives and what needs direct validation.

## Outputs

Company dossier; entity map; factual timeline; competitive position; risks; key unanswered questions; source register.

## Quality gates

- Do not treat investor deck or web page statements as independently verified.
- Distinguish similarly named entities.
- No assumed revenue, valuations, employee counts or customers.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/company-investigation.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
