# Market Landscape

**Purpose:** Research market structure and dynamics with explicit definitions, segmentation, competitive boundaries and factual uncertainty.

## When to use

New sector, market-entry choice, competitive benchmark, diligence, strategic positioning or market sizing.

## Inputs

Geography, timeframe, customer segment, category boundaries, units of measurement, primary and secondary sources.

## Method

1. Define market and substitutes in buyer terms; specify geography and time horizon.
2. Map value chain, competitors, incumbents, platforms, regulators, distribution, capital and ecosystem dependencies.
3. Construct top-down and bottom-up market estimates; reconcile denominators, methods and base years.
4. Separate established facts, analyst estimates, scenarios and assumptions; evaluate source incentives.
5. Identify whitespace hypotheses and changes that could invalidate the market definition.

## Outputs

Landscape map; market sizing ledger; source-backed competitor matrix; scenarios; strategic implications.

## Quality gates

- Never present TAM as obtainable revenue.
- No mixing currencies or years without adjustment.
- Do not claim live verification without real source access.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/market-landscape.json` to test relevance, factual discipline and output usefulness against a no-skill baseline.
