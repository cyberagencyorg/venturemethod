# Venture Thesis

**Purpose:** Turn an opportunity into an investable, testable venture hypothesis with staged commitments and meaningful kill criteria.

## When to use

Founding, studio incubation, venture selection, portfolio incubation, spinout or commercial concept evaluation.

## Inputs

Opportunity evidence, market mechanics, proposed team and unfair advantage, channels, capital appetite, mission constraints.

## Method

1. Specify problem, customer, why now, differentiated insight, delivery mechanism and profit logic.
2. Compare build, buy, partner, license and do-nothing routes; expose opportunity cost.
3. Model core assumptions across desirability, viability, feasibility, defensibility, distribution and regulatory exposure.
4. Define explicit stage gates, experiment budget, time-to-learning and discontinuation conditions.
5. Write best-case, base-case and adverse narratives with traceable assumptions.

## Outputs

Venture thesis memo, wedge, staged capital plan, assumption register, decision criteria and milestone map.

## Quality gates

- A compelling story is not evidence of demand.
- No single point estimate for uncertain finance.
- Negative findings must remain visible to decision-makers.
- Label observations, sourced third-party assertions, analysis and recommendations separately.
- If required information is absent, disclose it and propose next checks; never fabricate completion.
- Prefer the shortest defensible workflow appropriate to the stakes.

## Handoff contract

Include engagement ID, objective, references to source IDs and claim IDs, unresolved issues, recommended next action, and approval owner.
