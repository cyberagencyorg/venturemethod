# Brand Architecture

**Purpose:** Connect entity identity, purpose, positioning, brand promise, offers, messages and channels into a coherent brand system.

## When to use

A company, founder or venture needs identity research, offer architecture, messaging, voice, content system or multi-brand relationships.

## Inputs

Entity and subbrand relationships, actual audience, business model, founder intent, existing copy/assets, research, constraints, channel inventory.

## Positioning and editorial skill boundaries

Read host-approved brand context first. Use `marketing-context` for durable prior decisions and exclusions; `competitive-positioning` for buyer alternatives and category selection; `marketing-copywriting` only after the commercial argument exists. This skill retains the higher-level entity architecture and channel consistency decision. Do not replace approved founder or institutional voice with generic agency language. See `docs/POSITIONING_COPY_AND_VOICE.md`.

## Method

1. Separate identity, ontology of the enterprise, market positioning, visual expression and editorial voice before producing copy.
2. Identify beneficiaries, jobs, proof, competitors/substitutes, actual offer capacity and trust barriers.
3. Resolve parent/subbrand architecture and cross-channel roles; do not collapse a client campaign into studio or holding-company identity.
4. Produce clear positioning alternatives with what each emphasizes and sacrifices, tested against buyers rather than founder taste alone.
5. Map offer-to-audience-to-content-to-channel-to-conversion connections and author claims that can be substantiated.
6. Specify reuse rules for creative systems, examples, accessibility and required approvals.

## Outputs

Brand architecture map; positioning alternatives; voice and messaging guide; offer hierarchy; audience-channel-content map; creative handoff.

## Quality gates

- Do not invent customer proof or brand permissions.
- Distinguish owner identity from acquired clients and unrelated ventures.
- No generic marketing language substituted for strategic clarity.
- Brand system is usable across actual channels and deliverable types.
- Prefer evidence-linked, economical work proportionate to actual decision risk.

## Handoff contract

State engagement ID, bounded objective, source and claim IDs, open assumptions, options, reviewer, approval owner, and next test.

## Evaluation

Use `evals/brand-architecture.json` with representative, ambiguous, and adversarial cases against a no-skill baseline. Structural checks alone are not effectiveness proof.
