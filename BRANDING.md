# Venture Method identity and editorial standard

**Name:** Venture Method

**Created by:** Brad Khan

**Canonical repository:** `cyberagencyorg/venturemethod` (private during development)

**Short description:** Brad Khan's adaptive, AI-native, agent-compatible framework for research, strategy, product leadership, venture building, and organizational intelligence.

**Tagline:** The right methods. Better decisions. Knowledge that keeps up.

## Who it serves

Founders and solo venture architects first; also CEOs, CPOs, venture studios, family offices, investors, researchers, and advisors. Examples should demonstrate cross-disciplinary questions, not just software product delivery.

## How to describe it responsibly

Venture Method is a portable framework that organizes practical methods and evidence-linked work across an engagement's life. Its local CLI supports explicit decisions and revisions. The method catalog and portable skills are starting points, not demonstrated substitutes for experienced specialists.

Do not say the prototype autonomously researches, runs agents, verifies claims, generates investment-grade reports, or provides multi-user access. Those are goals, not present capabilities.

## Brand boundaries

- Venture Method is the framework, not a portal or client platform.
- Cyber Agency is the current hosting organization, not a mandatory dependency.
- Humanscaled, Open ASS and Creative Studio may consume or complement approved outputs; none is required.
- Use a confident, literate and grounded editorial voice. Explain what a method actually does and how it helps decide.
- Public releases require license, third-party notices, private-data review, explicit evaluation limits and a tested demonstration.
